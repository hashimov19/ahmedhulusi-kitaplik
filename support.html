Ahmed Hulûsi Library — Support

A free, ad-free app for reading, searching and taking notes on the works Ahmed Hulûsi offers free of charge at ahmedhulusi.org — offline.

Help and feedback: hashimova@gmail.com

Frequently asked

On first launch it says "Setting up the library" and waits

All 49 works (about 48 MB) are installed onto the phone the first time you open the app. It can take up to half a minute depending on the device, and happens only once. If it seems stuck, close and reopen the app; it restarts the installation cleanly rather than leaving a half-copied file behind.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. Every book, the search index, the glossary and the surah list are inside the app. The internet is needed only to stream or download an audiobook.

Where are my notes stored?

On your phone only. Nothing is sent to any server. See the Privacy Policy.

If I change phones, do I lose my notes?

Not if you back them up. Settings → Back up writes a file you can save anywhere (iCloud Drive, Google Drive, email). On the new phone use Settings → Restore from a backup and pick the file. Restoring adds to your notes; it never deletes them.

How do I select a sentence?

Tap a word: the sentence is highlighted and a handle appears at each end. Drag the handles left, right, up or down to extend the selection — including into the paragraph above or below. Tapping an empty area clears it.

Audio keeps playing after I leave the player — how do I stop it?

On the Library, Search, Notes and Settings screens a bar sits just above the tab bar. The pause button there is enough. Tapping the bar returns you to the player; the × stops playback entirely.

Why is the interface still in the other language?

There are two separate settings. Settings → App language controls the menus. The Türkçe/English chip in the Library only chooses which language's works are listed; it does not touch the menus.

I deleted the audiobooks but no space was freed

Settings → Downloaded audiobooks really removes them from the device. The phone's own storage screen sometimes keeps showing the old figure for a while.

I found a mistake in one of the works

The texts are taken verbatim from the publications on ahmedhulusi.org. If you spot a transcription error, write with the book title and chapter and we will correct it.

About the works

Every work in this app belongs to Ahmed Hulûsi and is offered free of charge with the author's permission, provided the source is credited. It may never be sold; the app carries no ads and costs nothing.
