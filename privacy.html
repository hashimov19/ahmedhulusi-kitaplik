Privacy Policy — Ahmed Hulûsi Library

Last updated: 6 October 2026

The short version

This app collects nothing from you. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server of ours. Where you left off, the notes you take, the passages you highlight and your settings all stay on your own phone and are never sent anywhere. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

The easiest way to verify this: put the phone in airplane mode. The library, search, your notes and the glossary keep working — everything except downloading an audiobook. None of it is connected to anything.

What stays on your device

All of the following is stored in a local database on your phone:

  • The passages you highlight, your notes and your tags
  • Your bookmarks
  • Where you left off in each book
  • Your reading settings (theme, text size, line spacing, language)
  • Any audiobook files you download

You can delete these from inside the app. Removing the app deletes all of it from your phone. If you want a backup, Settings → Back up creates a file — and that file goes wherever you choose (iCloud Drive, Google Drive, email, anywhere). We never see it.

The one place the app reaches the internet

Audiobooks. The audio files are not bundled with the app; together they run to several gigabytes. When you play or download an audiobook, your phone fetches the file directly from ahmedhulusi.org. As with any file download on the internet, that site's server sees the request. We keep no record of these requests, and that server is not ours.

If you never play an audiobook, the app never reaches the internet — apart from the update check below.

App updates

On launch the app asks Expo's servers (expo.dev) whether a new interface update is available. That request carries only the app's version and platform; nothing that identifies you. Expo's privacy policy: https://expo.dev/privacy

Notifications

If you turn on the "Passage of the day" notification, it is scheduled locally on your phone. No server is involved and you are not registered with any push service. The passage is chosen from the books already on your device. You can turn it off in Settings at any time.

What we do not collect

To be explicit: no name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifier, advertising identifier, usage statistics or crash reports. The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising network and no crash-reporting service.

We do not sell, share or use any data for advertising — because there is no data to share.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and since it collects no data from anyone of any age, it collects none from children either.

Permissions

  • Notifications — requested only if you turn on "Passage of the day". Optional.
  • Storage — to save audiobooks to the phone and to write a backup file.

Camera, microphone, location, contacts and photo library access are never requested.

About the works

The works in this app belong to Ahmed Hulûsi and are offered free of charge at ahmedhulusi.org. The app presents them unchanged, with the source credited. The app is never sold and carries no advertising.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. If anything ever required us to start collecting data, we would say so plainly inside the app.
